# Project Overview

Assassin's Creed Shadows takes players to feudal Japan, delivering an expansive open-world action RPG experience filled with stealth, exploration, cinematic storytelling, and intense combat. Explore beautifully crafted landscapes, infiltrate heavily guarded castles, uncover historical conspiracies, and master two unique protagonists with distinct playstyles.

Featuring dynamic seasons, advanced environmental interactions, realistic lighting, and seamless world exploration, the game offers countless opportunities for strategic infiltration, fast-paced action, and immersive role-playing. Every mission encourages players to adapt tactics, utilize the environment, and choose between silent assassinations or direct confrontation.

Players will discover ancient temples, bustling towns, hidden villages, mountain fortresses, forests, and historical landmarks while progressing through an engaging narrative inspired by one of Japan's most fascinating historical periods.

# Stealth and Combat

Assassin's Creed Shadows combines classic stealth gameplay with fluid melee combat. Players can eliminate targets silently, utilize rooftops and shadows for infiltration, or engage enemies directly using swords, ranged weapons, and specialized abilities.

Each protagonist offers unique skills, combat techniques, equipment, and progression paths, allowing players to approach missions using different strategies depending on the situation.
